Case-specific build for beta=1.4 lm/merger study.
TFRAME controls 2D xcut/zcut; column 2 of the Movie details row controls
TFRAME_ME for 3D movie output (fallback: TFRAME when missing or non-positive).
Both intervals and filenames are indexed by nint(time/interval).
zcut includes Sal=coarse-grid buoyancy and Qv=coarse-grid vapor, plus
Vx, Vy, Vz and Pr. Reconstruct m anomaly from Sal + gamma*Qv at fixed z.
Full z-dependent m is Sal + gamma*Qv for this buoyancy formulation.
TSCALE_DIAG controls aggregation scale output independently.
